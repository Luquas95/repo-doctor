# forge-archived-active – Archived repo with local activity

**Category:** Hosting · **Severity:** MED · **Automatic fix:** no

## What the check looks for

The repo is archived on the hosting (read only), but locally it has commits newer than the last activity on the hosting, or unpushed commits.

## Why it matters

The work cannot be pushed and is easily lost.

## Steps

1. Decide: unarchive the repo on the hosting, or move the work to another repo.
