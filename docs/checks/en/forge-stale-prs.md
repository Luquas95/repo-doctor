# forge-stale-prs – Inactive pull requests and issues

**Category:** Hosting · **Severity:** LOW · **Automatic fix:** no

## What the check looks for

Open PRs/MRs and issues without activity for longer than `limits.stale_pr_days` (default 30). Informational.

## Why it matters

Hanging PRs go stale and pile up conflicts.

## Steps

1. Finish, close or comment on them.
